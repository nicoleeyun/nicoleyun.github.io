Violinist

Since the age of 5, violinist Nicole Yun, 23, has performed internationally as a soloist and passionate chamber and orchestra musician. She has made her presence performing at notable venues such as Carnegie Hall, Alice Tully Hall, Elbphilharmonie, Berlin Philharmonie, and the Musikverein, and has been awarded top prizes at competitions around the U.S.A. — the Overall Prize and DVC Philharmonic Award at the DVC Young Artist String Competition, first place awards at the American Protégé International Concerto Competition in Carnegie Hall, United States International Music Competition, Music in the Vineyards Competition, and The Korean-American Music Supporters Association Competition.

In addition, she has won prizes from the United States Open Music Competition, VOCE State Finals Competition, Coltman Chamber Music Competition, Korea Times competitions, and Fremont Symphony Orchestra Young Recitalist Competition.

Yun is a co-founding member of the Karuna Quartet based at the Juilliard School. Gold medalists and Composer Prize winners of the 2026 St. Paul String Quartet Competition, they made their Lincoln Center debut at Alice Tully Hall in 2026. She has attended the renowned Perlman Music Program Chamber Workshop as quartet in residence, and will be performing in the PMP Sarasota Suncoast series this November. In her studies, Yun has collaborated and worked under several acclaimed ensembles, including the Brentano, Dover, Juilliard, and Parker Quartets. Furthermore, she has studied chamber music with Catherine Cho, Laurie Smukler, Merry Peckham, and Natasha Brofsky.

Throughout the past several years, Yun has received fellowships to several chamber and orchestra festivals, such as the Norfolk Chamber Music Festival, Bowdoin International Music Festival, Orford Music Academy, Vivace Music Festival, and Aspen Music Festival. In 2024, Yun was a full scholarship fellow at the Kent Blossom Music Festival, and performed with The Cleveland Orchestra as Assistant Concertmaster, alongside Peter Otto. As a former member of the San Francisco Symphony Youth Orchestra, Yun participated in the orchestra’s 2019 European tour, performing in prestigious venues around Europe.

Yun earned her Bachelor’s Degree at the Juilliard School, and is currently pursuing her Master’s Degree under Catherine Cho. Her former teachers include Donald Weilerstein, Wei He, Ian Swensen, Eric Chin, and Li Lin.

Private violin lessons

I believe in thoughtful, individualized instruction that helps each student develop strong technique, musicianship, confidence, and a lasting connection to music.

Nicole has taught students in New York City and Australia, including students enrolled in Juilliard Pre-College and the Mannes School of Music. She has also served as a Violin Sectional Coach for the Juilliard Pre-College Orchestra. She emphasizes efficient practice strategies, giving her students the tools to practice confidently and independently.
